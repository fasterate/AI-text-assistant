2. Information processed and its sources

Information on your device. The Windows application stores model settings, prompts, conversation text, and images included in conversations on your device. API Keys are encrypted locally using Windows system protection capabilities. Conversation text, images, and ordinary settings are not all encrypted. We do not operate application account registration, login, or a cloud synchronization backend. During ordinary use, these local materials are not automatically uploaded to the operator.

Model requests. When you request generation or start a conversation, relevant text, images, and completed conversation history that remains on your device may be sent as context to your configured model endpoint, through your configured proxy if any. Requests include an API Key or other required authentication information. Connection tests also send a short test instruction and authentication information to the selected endpoint. Screenshot text recognition runs locally; when you choose to send an image, that image is sent to the model service. Reading selected text may temporarily use the clipboard and attempt to restore it; generated results may also be written to the clipboard at your direction.

Purchases and support. When you purchase or contact support, we process the email address, order identifier, amount, currency, payment status, messages, feedback, and attachments you provide. Do not send support a complete card number, card security code, API Key, or unrelated sensitive information. Website hosting may also generate visitor records such as IP addresses, as explained in Section 4.

Protect your device, email, and keys, and avoid unnecessary sensitive information in conversations or support requests. Local conversations and images are not fully encrypted. If a personal information security incident occurs, we will take appropriate response measures and notify affected individuals and competent authorities as required by applicable law.
